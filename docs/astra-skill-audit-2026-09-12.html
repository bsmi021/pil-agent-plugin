Guidance applied

Astra guidanceFindingUpdate
Keep activation descriptions short and discriminating.Descriptions ranged from 35 to 102 words and enumerated many capabilities.Reduced them to 19–31 words with task boundaries that distinguish pixel, combined, reconstruction, and setup work.
Use progressive disclosure.image-measurement loaded a 610-line reference manual for every invocation.Replaced it with a 90-line decision router. Exact commands come from pil_capabilities.py; advanced modes route to existing workflow and metric documentation only when needed.
Avoid elaborate recipes where the model can choose.Several skills prescribed long sequential methods and repeated command catalogs.Kept decision criteria and stage gates, but removed repeated examples and implementation history. Fixed sequences remain only where calibration, mutation, or refusal correctness depends on order.
Keep boundaries proportional.Safety and evidence limits were mixed with defensive wording and duplicated background.Retained the real boundaries—installation authorization, no geometry from pixels, explicit solve gates, refusal states, and non-overwriting Blender edits—while removing generic caution.
Define completion and avoid unnecessary testing/work.The skills were precise about operations but less explicit about when the requested outcome was complete.Added bounded finish conditions and told the measurement skill not to run unrelated metrics merely because they exist.
